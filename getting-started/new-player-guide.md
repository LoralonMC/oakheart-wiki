# New Player Guide

Welcome to Oakheart! This guide covers your first moments on the server and help you get established in the survival world.

---

## Your First Minutes

### Spawning at the Oak Tree

When you join Oakheart, you'll appear at the base of a massive oak tree in the center of our medieval village. This is Oakheart's heart—the central hub where all adventures begin.

You'll also find a **Welcome Book** in your inventory — a clickable in-game guide with the most useful commands and where to find things. Open it any time you need a quick reference.

You also spawn with a **starter kit**: cottage armor, tools, food, torches, and a bed — enough to survive your first night.

### Elder Oakworth's Welcome Walk

A few seconds after you arrive, **Elder Oakworth**, the village elder, walks over to greet you and offers to show you around. Say yes and he leads you on a short stroll through the village (about 3 minutes): Toby's toy shop, the market, the vote crate and crates, Hammerhand's forge and the Village Quest Board, with the villagers saying hello as he passes. Just follow him; a trail of golden sparkles shows the way, and he waits if you fall behind.

The walk ends at the **Wild Portal**, where he hands you your starting gifts:

- {{item:emerald:16}}
- 1x {{item:claim_chunk}}

Prefer to explore on your own? Pick **"I'll find my own way"** and he gives you the Claim Chunk straight away. You can take the walk any time later: right-click Elder Oakworth beneath the oak and choose **"Walk the village again"**.

{% hint style="info" %}
On **Bedrock**, the Elder's lines appear in chat while you walk, so nothing blocks your view.
{% endhint %}

### First Roots

Once you step through the portal, a short quest appears in your sidebar to help you settle in:

1. **Claim land, or join a friend's.** Your own claim or being trusted on someone else's land both count.
2. **Set a home there** with `/sethome`.
3. **Place your bed.**

Do them in any order. When all three are done, the Elder sends his congratulations. There's no reward: a protected base, a home and a respawn point are the reward.

{% hint style="info" %}
**Need a quick reference?** Run `/tutorial` anytime to reopen your **Welcome Book**.
{% endhint %}

## Getting to the Survival World

The **Wild Portal** at the north edge of spawn is your gateway to the wilderness. You have two options:

| Method | How |
|--------|-----|
| Portal | Walk through the Wild Portal |
| Command | Type `/wild` |

This teleports you to a random location in the survival world where you can start building.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Tip:** Once you find a spot you like, claim it and set a home right away so you can return!
{% endhint %}

## Protecting Your Build

### Creating a Land Claim

Oakheart uses a chunk-based claiming system. To protect your builds:

1. **Stand where you want to build**
2. **Create your land:** `/lands create <name>`
   *Example:* `/lands create MyBase`
3. **Claim the chunk:** `/lands claim`
4. **View your claim:** `/lands view`

### Claim Limits

| Source | Chunks |
|--------|--------|
| Starting amount | 4 chunks |
| Playtime bonus (1 per hour for your first 4 hours, automatic) | +4 chunks |
| **Total free chunks** | **8 chunks** |

The playtime chunks land in your inventory automatically as you play — no command needed. After the first 4 hours the automatic bonus stops, and you earn additional chunks through voting, crates, ranks, and events.

{% hint style="success" %}
**What's protected?**
Within your claim, only you and trusted players can build, break blocks, open containers, or harm your animals.
{% endhint %}

## Setting Homes

Homes let you quickly teleport back to your favorite locations.

### Basic Commands

| Command | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| `/sethome <name>` | Save your current location |
| `/home <name>` | Teleport to a saved home |
| `/homes` | Open the homes GUI menu |
| `/removehome <name>` | Delete a home |

### Home Limits

You start with **1 home** at the default rank (Meadow). As you rank up through voting or purchasing store ranks, you unlock more:

- **Rank 1 (Meadow):** 1 home
- **Rank 10 (Hollow):** 10 homes
- **Rank 20 (Heartwood):** 15 homes

## The Economy

### Earning Emeralds

Emeralds are Oakheart's currency. The primary way to earn them is by selling items at spawn shops:

| Shop | What to Sell |
|------|-------------|
| **Miner (Stonebeard)** | Ores, gems, stone, redstone |
| **Lumberjack (Willowbark)** | Logs, charcoal, bee nests, sticks, apples |
| **Farmer (Seedsower)** | Crops, honey, berries |
| **Alchemist (Moonwhisper)** | Mob drops, brewing materials |
| **Fisherman (Tidecaller)** | Fish, ocean treasures |

Each shop has **daily trade limits** per player—this keeps the economy balanced. Limits reset daily.

You can also earn emeralds from **[quests](../features/quests.md)**: the villagers post daily and weekly requests on the Village Quest Board, each paying emeralds, experience, and a Bounty Satchel of surprises.

### Spending Emeralds

- **Set player warps** (each one after the first costs more than the last, from {{item:emerald:24}} up)
- **Rent sponsored warp slots** (premium visibility)
- **Buy Emerald Crate keys** ({{item:emerald:576}} each)
- **Trade with players** for items

## Voting

Voting supports the server and earns you rewards!

### How to Vote

1. Run `/vote` to open the voting GUI
2. Click each of the 5 voting sites
3. Vote on each site (takes ~2 minutes total)

### Vote Rewards (Per Site)

- **1** {{item:vote_token}} — Trade with Merchant Bramblewood
- **1** {{item:votekey}} — Opens the Vote Crate
- **1 Vote Point** — Progress toward vote ranks

Vote daily on all 5 sites = **5 keys, 5 tokens, 5 points per day!**

## Helpful Tips

### Do's and Don'ts

**DO:**
- Claim your builds immediately
- Explore spawn to learn the layout
- Ask questions in chat—we're friendly!
- Vote daily for free rewards
- Trade safely with `/trade <player>`

**DON'T:**
- Build near others without asking (64+ blocks distance)
- Destroy unclaimed builds (still against rules!)
- Use hacked clients or exploits
- Share personal information

### Common Mistakes to Avoid

1. **Forgetting to claim** — Builds outside claims can be griefed
2. **Getting lost** — Set a home as soon as you find a spot!
3. **Selling everything** — Keep materials for your own projects
4. **Ignoring voting** — Free rewards add up fast over time
