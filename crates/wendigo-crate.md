# Wendigo Crate

The Wendigo Crate is Oakheart's Halloween 2026 crate. Its centrepiece is the **Wendigo set**: frost-bitten bone and antler wraps for every tool and weapon, the Wendigo Elytra, a chat tag and a join/leave message. The set was made for this year only and will never come back in any later crate.

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Keys are sold until November 15.** After that the crate retires, but it never stops working: any Wendigo Key you hold opens it forever, and the crate keeps exactly the rewards it has today.
{% endhint %}

---

## How to Get Keys

Wendigo Keys are sold on the [server store](https://oakheart.net/store) only. They don't drop from votes, other crates or anywhere in-game.

| Pack | Price | Per Key |
|------|-------|---------|
| 3x Wendigo Keys | $20 | $6.67 |
| 5x Wendigo Keys | $25 | $5.00 |
| 10x Wendigo Keys | $40 | $4.00 |

Keys are physical items. You can trade them, and they never expire.

---

## Where to Open

Open it at `/warp crates`, next to the Daisy, Rose and Lily crates. Left-click the crate to preview everything inside.

---

## What's Inside

```crate
WendigoCrate
```

{% hint style="info" %}
**Every reward has the same chance.** Each of the 30 rewards is a 1 in 30 roll (3.33%). Over a 5-key pack, any one reward you're hoping for turns up about 16% of the time.
{% endhint %}

---

## What Comes Back, and What Doesn't

| Never comes back | Returns in future Halloween crates |
|------------------|------------------------------------|
| The Wendigo set: 12 wraps, Wendigo Elytra, chat tag, join/leave message and the Wendigaunt pet | Every Halloween plushie bundle, including the Limited 2025 and Limited 2026 bundles |
| The Wendigo Lure, and whatever it calls | The Adorable, Graveyard and Trick or Treat bundles, the Macabre booster and the Halloween companions |

Each Halloween crate carries every Halloween collectible released so far, so a plushie you miss this year can still turn up next year. Each crate's themed set is made once and never appears again. Claim chunks and crate crystals are available all year anyway.

| Item | What it is |

---

## The Wendigo Lure

The crate also holds a {{item:wendigo_lure}}. What it calls, and what it leaves behind, is for you to find out.

- It only works **outdoors, at night**, outside any claim and away from spawn. If a condition isn't met, the lure stays in your inventory.
- Bring friends.
- Lures keep working after November 15.

---

## After November 15

Keys come off the store, but the crate stays at `/warp crates` and keeps working with exactly these rewards. Next year's Halloween crate gets its own new set; nothing from the Wendigo set will be in it.
